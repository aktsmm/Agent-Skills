# Inbox

Read [the workflow](references/workflow.md) before updating the owning workspace.

- Preserve source wording except credentials and token-bearing URLs; record omissions without secret values.
- Do not treat a question or an ordinary conversation as permission to create records.
- Link existing meeting records instead of duplicating their full content in the inbox.
- Keep ambiguous workstream matches as candidates until the name and scope are confirmed.

Done means the monthly entry and any unambiguous workstream update are saved without duplicate records.
