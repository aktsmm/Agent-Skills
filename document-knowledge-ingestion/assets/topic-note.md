# Topic

## Evidence and Reading Scope

| Document ID         | Original section / units                | Actually read   | Omitted or uncertain                                    |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Replace from ledger | Named heading and line/page/slide range | Full or partial | Text, images, notes, appendices, or videos not reviewed |

State the source version and link to saved evidence. Distinguish the reading date from the source's publication date and any later fact-check date.

## Source Explanation

For each substantive section, provide its source ID and exact units, then explain:

- The problem and the causal mechanism or reasoning.
- Decision criteria and when the approach does not apply.
- The source's procedure, inputs, expected result, and a specific example.
- Tradeoffs, failure cases, uncertainty, and prerequisites.

Use concise paraphrase rather than large quotations. Preserve operational detail; do not replace reasoning with a list of topic names.

## Cross-Source Synthesis

Explain agreements and contradictions, distinguishing independent evidence from revisions or generated copies. Label your interpretations rather than attributing them to a source.

## Proposed Applications

Keep untested examples, new exercises, and recommended changes separate from source claims. Do not imply an original example was executed during this reading pass.

## Unresolved Work

Identify unread ranges, unavailable artifacts, OCR ambiguities, unstable product facts, and reuse permissions. Give a concrete resume condition for each blocker.
